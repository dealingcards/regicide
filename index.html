Regicide

A solo battle against twelve corrupted royals.

The court has fallen to darkness. Armed with a humble tavern deck of numbered cards, you must slay all 4 Jacks, then 4 Queens, then 4 Kings. Each card you play deals damage equal to its value — and its suit grants a power.

♥
Hearts — heal: shuffle that many cards from the discard back under the tavern deck.
♦
Diamonds — draw that many cards (up to a hand of 8).
♣
Clubs — double damage.
♠
Spades — shield: reduce the royal's attack by that value, for as long as it lives.

But beware: every royal is immune to its own suit's power, and after you strike, it strikes back — you must discard cards worth at least its attack, or fall.

You carry two Jesters: flip one to discard your whole hand and draw 8 fresh cards. And the Jester on your right will whisper tips as you learn. Good hunting.

The daily castle is the same shuffle for every player — compare results with friends.

How to play

Your turn, in order

  • 1. Play cards (you may first flip a Jester to refresh your hand).
  • 2. Suit powers resolve — hearts heal, diamonds draw, clubs double, spades shield.
  • 3. Damage equal to the cards' total value hits the royal.
  • 4. The royal strikes back — discard cards from your hand totalling at least its attack (minus spade shields). If you can't, you lose.

What you may play

  • A single card of any value.
  • An Ace + one other card ("animal companion") — values add, both suits trigger.
  • A set of 2s, 3s, 4s, or 5s of the same rank, if the total is 10 or less (e.g. three 3s = 9). All suits trigger.

The royals

  • Jack: 20 health, 10 attack. Queen: 30 / 15. King: 40 / 20.
  • Each royal is immune to its own suit — that suit's power never triggers against it. (Spades royals are the nastiest: nothing softens their attack.)
  • Defeat a royal with exact damage and it lands face-up on top of the tavern deck — you'll draw it and can wield it as a mighty weapon (J = 10, Q = 15, K = 20).
  • Otherwise it goes to the discard pile. Either way, you skip its counterattack and face the next royal.

Jesters

  • You have 2 Jesters. Flip one — at the start of your turn, or just before taking a royal's counterattack — to discard your whole hand and draw 8 fresh cards. This doesn't count as "drawing", so it works even against Diamonds royals.
  • Finish with both Jesters untouched for a Gold victory; one flipped is Silver, both is Bronze.
  • (In multiplayer Regicide, Jesters instead cancel a royal's immunity — solo plays by its own rules.)
  • There is no yielding in solo: if you cannot play a card, you lose.
